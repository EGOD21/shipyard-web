The Human Condition: "The characteristics and key events of a human life."

*We* exist as individualized fragments of a collective existence, searching for something ambiguously meaningful and inarguably real.

In our quest we find that the "realest" of our findings is found to be in ourselves, experiencing the emotions (both painful and pleasurable) alongside our peers amidst both individualized and collective experiences. 

Aiming to satisfy our own selfish and confusing desires and aspirations without confessing so.

Yet we still search, may we find what is real. The truth by which our own existence is subtly yet masterfully aligned with.
