The observation of existence itself allows us the ability to be.

Since we were born we began the intake of data around us, supplied by our senses and informing our perceptions. Each entity doing the same uncontrolled infusion of the world around them into their innermost being.

But when we lay our heads at night and lose connection to this reality we call our own, reality as we know it individually becomes devoid of existence and we disappear into our own sub-conscious full of often perverted and intertwined memories of our subjective experience of the supposedly objective reality.

Because of this assumption, reality as we know it and our conscious experience seems to be completely reliant on each of our individual observations and perceptions.

May we, dear fellow traversers of this confusing existence continue to observe, perceive, and just BE. For this opportunity is what drives our reality.
