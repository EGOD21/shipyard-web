"Knowledge speaks, but wisdom listens." - Jimi Hendrix

The ability to *know* is ever present and every growing, facts flow through our fingerprints at every hour of every day and yet despite us *knowing*, the majority of those that walk amongst us are *lost* in the ever flowing current of data. 

Wisdom is the adhesion mechanism by which existence as a whole and interactions as a part, intertwine and bind amongst themselves. Allowing application of *what* we know to *who* we are and *what* we do.

The success you may find, dear reader. Is largely dependent on the wisdom you seek, though by man or God you may find the mechanism by which the knowledge you've acquired can be used.
