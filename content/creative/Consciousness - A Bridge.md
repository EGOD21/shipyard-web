"Consciousness": The state of being awake and aware of one's surroundings, thoughts, feelings, and internal experiences.

The mechanism by which us as humans live, observe, experience, navigate life as we currently know it. Sometimes taking us deeper then we ever thought possible, other times keeping us at distance from what we are searching for.

An interesting mechanism indeed, allowing us to completely remove ourself from reality every night when we sleep. Creating it's own version of the reality it experienced autonomously. 

Easily corruptible just the same, though by our developmental environment, or our current one. Some never *achieving* the blissful observations we all should.

May you, dear reader. Experience the lustrous and mysterious depths that is the observational ability of our human mind. Consciousness.
