"Pride": the quality or state of being proud: such as a reasonable self-esteem confidence and satisfaction in oneself.

Interwoven into the nature of our own existence is pride. Constant assumptions that our observations, experiences, and opinions are canonical to the human condition.

Even amidst emotional contradictions within ourself, we find our path through this world is the correct one, leading to a dangerous level of cognitive dissonance related to our connections with others. 

May we continue to be broken and and encouraged to grow, finding our experiences simply scratch the surface of the iceberg that is human existence.
