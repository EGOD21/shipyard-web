"Hopelessness": The feeling or condition of having no hope.

Hopelessness is dangerous rabbit hole, relying on what we can touch to save us from what can touch us.

 All beings existing in their own right in this physical world, feeling their own scent of hopelessness, longing for something (or someone) to save them from the evils of the earth.

How then can a being in this plane of reality save another in the same?

May our hope and desire be set on something beyond the physical, in it's own right impossible of being hopeless.
