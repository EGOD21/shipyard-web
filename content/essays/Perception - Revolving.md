"Perception": The process of using our senses—like sight, hearing, and touch—to notice, organize, and interpret information from the world around us.

The Idea of perception is derived from our understanding of the perception of others, being the ability to perceive that which happens to and around us while also attempting to be conscious of how others perceive their own picture.

This loop keeps people separated and confused, allowing one human to perceive and create their own beliefs and opinions based on the reality they consume, but often times forcing a peer to adapt their perception to that of which the first person claimed. 

Often the rewarded party is that by which can articulate the picture they see and emotions they feel the clearest (or loudest).

May we, dear fellow traversers of this confusing existence allow our perceptions of reality to be shaped and stirred by that of our peers.
