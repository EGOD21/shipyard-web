“Human Experience”: Behaviors (perceiving, doing, feeling) or events (happenings) that people are aware of, whether individually or collectively.

There is approx. 8.3 Billion people walking the face of this planet, hurdling through the expanses of space. Each experiencing daily what they deem as "real". Basing their definition of "real" on what they observe from those around them, sparsely realizing the expression they output into the world becomes the input for a peer.

Human experience as a whole seems to be the collective expressions of individuals, coming together to create the fabric of the reality influencing us individually. And it's this revolving scenario that allows all, the opportunity of experience.

May we, dear fellow traversers of this confusing existence allow ourselves the intrinsic freedom of expression and contribute to the fullness of the human experience.
