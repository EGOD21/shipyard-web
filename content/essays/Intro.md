"**Shipyard**": a yard, place, or enclosure where ships are built or repaired.

The idea of a shipyard is for a ship to periodically stop and "rest" for sake of routine maintenance, unplanned repairs, or major retrofits. 

In the same way I intend this shipyard to be a place for the weary traverser of the internet's infinite passages to stop, learn, repair, and grow. 

The "**Shipyard**" intends to be a space of stored thoughts, theories, and questions that intend to someday go out to the place or person it was intended for.
